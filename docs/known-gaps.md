# Known gaps

Every known limitation of the agent as built on 19 Oct 2026 (after milestone 9c; updated from the list after milestone 9b, tag `milestone-9b`), in one place. Each row names the gap, why it exists (a decision, a cut, or a piece not yet built), what the agent does today when it meets the situation, what would close it, and where it is written down. Nothing here moves money: every gap ends in a question, a refusal from the resource file, or a card that still needs the Confirm tap (rule 1 of `CLAUDE.md`).

Sources: `docs/architecture.md` (the decisions and its own "Known gaps, reported on purpose" table), `docs/build-plan.md`, `docs/disclosure.md`, `docs/tuning-backlog.md`, `docs/matching-rules.md`, `docs/rules-coverage.md`, `docs/failure-modes.md`, the "Known limits" and "Known gaps" tables of `docs/agent-core.md` and `docs/agent-web.md`, the security reviews under `docs/reviews/` and the close-out plans under `docs/plans/`. No row is added that those files do not record, with one exception marked as such (row 24, from Khalil's brief of 19 Oct 2026).

Reading the columns:

- **Why**: *decision* means a settled design choice (D1 to D22 in the architecture, or a rule Khalil agreed); *cut* means an item on the cut list that was dropped or narrowed; *not yet built* means the plan names the milestone; *awaiting Khalil* means only he can supply it; *measured* means a model behaviour seen in the development-set runs and left for the milestone 10 pass.
- **Today**: what the user sees. String names are keys in `src/Agent.Web/Resources/PhoneStrings.resx`; learning-log reasons are the values pinned in `data/seed/settings.json`.
- Row numbers are kept when a row is removed, because other files cite rows by number: row 17 (compound requests, milestone 9c) was removed on 19 Oct 2026 when 9c built them, and row 8 now records the cap as built.
- Correct refusals are not gaps. A request to receive money from a contact (`receive_from`), another person's balance (`other_balance`), a cash withdrawal (`withdrawal`), or a message typed as an instruction to the agent (the red-team cases) is refused by design and is not listed here.

## What is not a gap

- **A security review finding that was fixed** is recorded in its review and in `docs/build-plan.md`, not here.
- **Rows of `docs/failure-modes.md` marked "review only"** are coverage notes on a handled path, not open behaviour.
- **Faults 1, 2 and 4 of slide 2 have no switch to inject** because a double tap and a repeated request are user actions, held by development cases and tests.
- **`pin_locked` and `caller_cancelled` are provisional reason codes** kept by Khalil's decision of 14 Oct 2026.
- **Two development-set keys that could not pass as written** (ADD-13, ADD-15) were settled by Khalil's rulings of 16 and 17 Oct 2026 and pass now.
- **The typed answer read as unclear** (AMB-06, tuning backlog item 2) was closed by code on 17 Oct 2026: an answer to a pending question is read in code before any model call.
