## Rulings taken by the controller (for Khalil to confirm)

1. **Known provider names** are read from the wallet's biller list: the part of a biller's name in parentheses, Arabic (`اشتراك الانترنت (ايرثلنك)` gives `ايرثلنك`) or English (`Internet (Earthlink subscription)` gives `Earthlink`, compared without case); a biller whose names have no parentheses has none. `biller_text` contains a provider name when one of its words, after `ArabicText.Key` normalising (rule 1: a leading `ال` dropped), equals the provider name's key, or the English name without case. Recorded in `docs/matching-rules.md` (16/10/2026) as Claude's reading.
2. **Part of a bill** (`unsupported_reason` `part_of_bill`) is read as a bill request: the bill is resolved from `biller_code`, `biller_text` and `bill_label_text` as any bill is; one bill with an amount due (a `Card` resolution, or the internet bill of an `AskBillOrStore`, since the user means the bill) is refused `bills_paid_in_full` with the amount due and the detail `part_of_bill`; several bills ask `which_bill` and the tap gives the same refusal for the tapped bill; nothing due is `nothing_due`; no bill (and the store branch of rule 17) is `bill_not_found`. The learning log gets `part_of_bill` (the logged reason of `settings.json`). The reply text `Refusal_bills_paid_in_full` already carries the amount.
3. **The six failure modes of slide 2** are the six faults of `docs/matching-rules.md` ("Faults the switch must offer"): 1 the wallet pays but its answer times out; 2 the wallet times out before paying; 3 the wallet answers with an error; 4 the model cannot be reached; 5 the model's reply is not in the required structure; 6 money leaves the wallet from outside while a card waits. Double tap and a repeated request are user actions with their own rows.
4. **"The fault switch in settings, always at hand"** is built as its own panel in the always-visible trace column (above the live trace), so it is at hand whatever component the side menu shows; the settings component points to it in one line. The console's wallet admin key is `Console:WalletAdminKey` (`ConsoleOptions`, `Agent.Web`), default `dev` as the runner's, shown as `set` and never as a value.
5. **The Checking state resolves by itself:** while a card is Checking, the card panel sends a settle (`ChatSession.SettleAsync`, the same path as the check-again button, the same key) every `CheckingRetrySeconds` (5, editable in settings) until the record leaves Checking. The runner keeps its own settle loop (`Eval:SettleAttempts`, `Eval:SettleDelayMs`).
6. **The lookup by key** is display: `ConfirmHandler.SettleCheckingAsync` reads `GET /transactions/by-key` first and traces `lookup_by_key` with `done`, `failed`, `unknown_key` or `unavailable`; the outcome still comes from the resend under the same key, as `docs/wallet-api.md` requires (a by-key 404 is not final).
7. **`biller_text` and the examples:** prompt version 3 adds the field's description and `"biller_text": null` to every example; no other line changes (the AMB-06 line of the tuning backlog stays in the backlog). No dev-set sentence enters the prompt.
